# 3. Nine focused phases

**Decision:** Organize the roadmap as 9 phases, each about one subject, with every topic in a named group. Concepts sit next to what they depend on: consistency and CAP after replication, consistent hashing with partitioning, event-driven architecture with messaging.

**Why:** The first version had a mixed final phase (AI, company write-ups, papers, interview practice) and a phase that joined distributed systems theory with operations. Some concepts also came before the topics needed to understand them. The site is for study, so interview practice material is left out.

**Costs:** One more phase than before, and some topic numbers changed. No articles existed yet, so no links broke.

**Alternatives:** Keep 8 phases (forces unrelated subjects together), split data and caching into separate phases (more phases for little gain, since collapsible groups keep long phases short on screen).
