# 3. Nine focused phases

**Decision:** Organize the roadmap as 9 phases, each about one subject, with every topic in a named group. Concepts sit next to what they depend on: consistency and CAP after replication, consistent hashing with partitioning, event-driven architecture with messaging.

**Why:** The first version had a mixed final phase (AI, company write-ups, papers, interview practice) and a phase that joined distributed systems theory with operations. Some concepts also came before the topics needed to understand them. The site is for learning concepts, so hands-on design exercises ("Design a ..." case studies, a method for solving them) and interview practice are left out.

**Costs:** One more phase than before, and some topic numbers changed. No articles existed yet, so no links broke.

**Alternatives:** Keep the case studies as a phase (useful for interview prep, but that is not the goal here), keep 8 phases (forces unrelated subjects together), split data and caching into separate phases (more phases for little gain, since collapsible groups keep long phases short on screen).
